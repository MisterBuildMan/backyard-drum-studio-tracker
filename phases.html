Project Phases

The drum studio build is organized into 10 phases, from initial planning through final setup.

11% Overall
Phase 0 In Progress

Project Setup

Establish documentation methodology, create templates and conventions.

Phase 1 In Progress

Planning & Permits

Site assessment, local code research, permit applications, budget planning.

Phase 2 Not Started

Design

3D modeling, floor plan, sound isolation strategy, electrical layout, materials selection.

Phase 3 Not Started

Site Prep & Foundation

Ground clearing and leveling, formwork, concrete slab pour, curing.

Phase 4 Not Started

Framing

Wall framing (double-stud for sound isolation), roof framing, door and window rough openings.

Phase 5 Not Started

Exterior

Sheathing, roofing, siding, exterior door and windows.

Phase 6 Not Started

Systems

Electrical rough-in, HVAC planning, soundproofing layers.

Phase 7 Not Started

Interior Finish

Insulation, drywall, flooring, interior door, trim and paint.

Phase 8 Not Started

Acoustic Treatment

Bass traps, absorption panels, diffusion (if needed).

Phase 9 Not Started

Final Setup

Equipment installation, final inspection, punch list completion.
